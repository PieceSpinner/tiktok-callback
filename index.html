TikTok Publishing Bot

A personal Telegram bot (@ttvssouybot) that lets its owner upload their own videos to their connected TikTok accounts through the official TikTok Content Posting API, and copy them to their own Telegram channels.

  1. The owner connects a TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit.
  2. The owner sends a video to the bot and picks the accounts.
  3. The bot uploads the video to TikTok (as an inbox draft or a direct post) and copies it to the linked Telegram channel.
