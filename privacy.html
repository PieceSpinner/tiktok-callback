Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This policy describes how the Telegram bot @ttvssouybot ("the service") handles data. The service is a personal publishing tool used only by its owner.

What we collect

  • TikTok: the account's open ID, access token and refresh token, received through TikTok Login Kit after you grant permission. We request only the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish. We never see your TikTok password.
  • Telegram: your Telegram user ID, the IDs and titles of channels you link, and the videos, photos and captions you send to the bot.

How we use it

Only to upload the content you send to the TikTok accounts and Telegram channels you choose, and to show you the result. We do not use the data for advertising, analytics or profiling.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored encrypted in a local database on the operator's own machine. Media files are processed in memory for the upload and are not kept after it finishes; Telegram keeps its own copies under Telegram's privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not sell or share data with anyone. Data is sent only to TikTok and Telegram, as needed to publish your content.

Retention and deletion

Account data is kept until you remove the account with the /accounts command in the bot, which deletes its tokens from the database. You can also revoke access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: github.com/PieceSpinner.
